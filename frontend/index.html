THE EMPTY-LOBBY PROBLEM

Getting a player to register
is only the beginning.

They arrive ready to play. But their preferred stakes are empty, the Sit & Go needs more players, and the next tournament still hasn’t filled.

For a new poker site, traffic is spread across games, stakes and time zones. A handful of players in one game does little for someone looking for another. That first visit can end before the first hand.

A FEW PRACTICAL QUESTIONS

Before the first hand.

Which poker platforms does PokerFill support?+

PokerFill supports EvenBet and Run Your Own Poker Site. On these platforms, you provide funded bot accounts and we help configure the connection, player profiles and traffic schedules.

Can PokerFill integrate with my custom poker site?+

Yes. We make new integrations straightforward and handle the technical setup for you. Share your platform details; we review its available interfaces, agree the integration scope and timing, then configure and test the connection before starting the trial.

Is PokerFill a complete product?+

Yes. PokerFill is a complete B2B poker bot and liquidity solution for poker site operators. Ready-made strategies, automated table filling, session scheduling, live monitoring and backoffice tools are included. You receive an existing management platform with ready-made strategies. You do not need to develop a poker strategy engine or build the management software yourself.

Do I need to develop an API integration?+

For supported platforms, no customer-developed API integration is required. Provide funded bot accounts and we help configure the connection, profiles and schedules. For an additional platform or custom site, we review the integration scope with you first.

Can bots run on our machines or yours?+

Both options are available. Bots can run on your Windows machines or on PokerFill-hosted machines. We agree hosting, capacity and setup with you before starting.

Who is PokerFill for?+

Poker site operators who need to support game availability while building their human player base, or fill gaps across particular games, stakes and hours.

Are these automated players?+

Yes. PokerFill manages poker bots with configurable strategy profiles and operating schedules. Operators should clearly disclose automated accounts to their players. Deployment must comply with applicable gaming regulations and platform rules, including those governing real-money play.

Can different games have different schedules?+

Yes. Cash games, Sit & Go and MTTs have separate weekly targets. Account settings and stake bands let you tune where your automated players participate.

Does every bot play the same way?+

No. Accounts can use different strategy profiles, betting styles, playing frequencies and session settings. You can build a varied player mix rather than one uniform style.

Can my team see and respond to table chat?+

Yes. Your team can read table chat, receive notifications of human messages, and instruct a selected bot to send a reply at its table.

How much does PokerFill cost?+

We provide a personal quote after discussing your platform, the number of bots you need, the games and stakes you want to support, and your traffic plan. Contact us with your requirements to discuss pricing.

Can I try PokerFill first?+

Yes. A one-month free trial is available after compatibility review. We agree the scope and start date before launch. You provide the bot accounts and fund their balances; those funds are separate from the free trial.

What do I need to provide to get started?+

Your poker platform details, a list of bot usernames for accounts created and funded on your site, and your preferred games, stakes and traffic plan. We confirm compatibility first, help with the connection, and use the backoffice CSV import to add the accounts.

How do I access the platform?+

Existing customers can use Customer sign in. Backoffice access is assigned by an administrator and scoped to your customer account and poker site.
