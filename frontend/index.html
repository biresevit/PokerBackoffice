A FEW PRACTICAL QUESTIONS

Before the first hand.

Who is PokerFill for?+

Poker site operators who need to support game availability while building their human player base, or fill gaps across particular games, stakes and hours.

Are these automated players?+

Yes. PokerFill manages poker bots with configurable strategy profiles and operating schedules. The operator controls their deployment and how automated players are presented to the site’s customers.

Can different games have different schedules?+

Yes. Cash games, Sit & Go and MTTs have separate weekly targets. Account settings and stake bands let you tune where your automated players participate.

Does every bot play the same way?+

No. Accounts can use different strategy profiles, betting styles, playing frequencies and session settings. You can build a varied player mix rather than one uniform style.

Can my team see and respond to table chat?+

Yes. Your team can read table chat, receive notifications of human messages, and instruct a selected bot to send a reply at its table.

How do I access the platform?+

Existing customers can use Customer sign in. Backoffice access is assigned by an administrator and scoped to your customer account and poker site.
